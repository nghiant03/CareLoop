## 2. Trend detection: Theil-Sen vs OLS vs EWMA

Synthetic 6-month K-like series (base 4.2, noise σ=0.05), 200 flat replications and 100 per other scenario; the trends.py flag rule (slope > 0.05/month or 3-month projection crossing) is applied identically to every estimator. EWMA = exponentially weighted least-squares slope (half-life 2 months).

| scenario | truth | theil-sen | ols | ewma |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| flat | no flag | end 0% · ever 8% | end 0% · ever 8% | end 0% · ever 8% |
| rising_slow | no flag | end 8% · ever 41% | end 8% · ever 41% | end 10% · ever 51% |
| falling | no flag | end 0% · ever 0% | end 0% · ever 0% | end 0% · ever 0% |
| plateau | no flag | end 14% · ever 96% | end 14% · ever 96% | end 6% · ever 97% |
| rising_fast | flag | end 98% · lead 2.77 mo | end 100% · lead 2.76 mo | end 100% · lead 2.74 mo |
| step_change | flag | end 100% · lead 0.61 mo | end 100% · lead 1.18 mo | end 100% · lead 1.22 mo |

| method | TP detection (end) ↑ | FP flat (end) ↓ | FP plateau (end) ↓ | FP all no-flag scenarios ↓ | mean lead (mo) ↑ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| theil-sen | 99% | 0% | 14% | 6% | 1.69 |
| ols | 100% | 0% | 14% | 6% | 1.97 |
| ewma | 100% | 0% | 6% | 4% | 1.98 |

*Reading the table:* for no-flag scenarios, `end` is the false-positive rate on the full 6-month series and `ever` adds transient mid-window alarms (the union over all prefixes). `plateau` is the adaptivity probe — a series that rose for 3 months then stabilised should **not** still be flagged at month 6; history-dominated estimators keep flagging it. For flag scenarios, `lead` is how many months before the end of the window the flag first fired; `step_change` is undetectable before the jump itself, so its lead is naturally small.

| method | flags on real cohort (40 series) | Jaccard vs theil-sen | mean |Δslope| vs theil-sen |
|---|---|---|---|
| theil-sen | 12 | 1.000 | 0.000 |
| ols | 13 | 0.786 | 0.009 |
| ewma | 14 | 0.733 | 0.017 |

The theil-sen column reproduces `trends.detect_trends` exactly: **True** (module reports 12 flags).

**Verdict — ewma.** ewma gives the best detection/robustness trade-off on the synthetic ground truth (TP detection 100%, flat FP 0%, plateau FP 6%, mean lead 1.98 months). The EWMA-weighted slope adapts fastest when a rise plateaus (Theil-Sen plateau FP 14% vs EWMA's lower rate) because it weights recent months, at some sensitivity to single-month spikes — a hybrid (flag on Theil-Sen, de-escalate on EWMA) is worth considering. On the real cohort it flags 14 series vs Theil-Sen's 12.

## 3. Substitution metrics: v1 euclidean vs renal-weighted vs v2 composite

Ranked candidate sets per query dish (214 queries with ≥2 compatible candidates). Renal weights (Na/K/P ×2): energy_kcal=1, protein_g=1, sodium_mg=2, potassium_mg=2, phosphorus_mg=2, carbs_g=1.

| metric pair | mean Kendall τ | min τ | max τ |
|---|---|---|---|
| v1_euclidean vs weighted_renal | 0.917 | 0.637 | 0.978 |
| v1_euclidean vs v2_composite | 0.914 | 0.711 | 1.000 |
| weighted_renal vs v2_composite | 0.868 | 0.434 | 0.965 |

| example dish | v1 euclidean top-3 | renal-weighted top-3 | v2 composite top-3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nasi Padang (rice + 3 dishes) (hawker) | Nasi Lemak with Fried Chicken; Mutton Briyani; Fried Hokkien Prawn Mee | Nasi Lemak with Fried Chicken; Mutton Briyani; Fried Hokkien Prawn Mee | Nasi Lemak with Fried Chicken; Mutton Briyani; Fried Hokkien Prawn Mee |
| Bandung (drink) | Milo (hot); Sugarcane Juice; Milo Dinosaur | Milo (hot); Sugarcane Juice; Teh (condensed milk) | Milo (hot); Sugarcane Juice; Teh (condensed milk) |
| Curry Fish Head (zi_char) | Assam Fish Head; Fish Head Soup; Fish Soup (fried fish) | Assam Fish Head; Fish Head Soup; Fish Soup (fried fish) | Assam Fish Head; Sambal Stingray; Salted Egg Prawns |

**Verdict — v2_composite.** All three metrics agree broadly (mean Kendall τ between 0.87 and 0.92 across pairs) — this dataset's six nutrients are correlated, so any sane nutrient distance produces a similar ordering. The renal-weighted distance is the closest to plain v1 euclidean (τ 0.92): weighting Na/K/P ×2 reshuffles the middle of the ranking but rarely the top-3 (the Nasi Padang and Bandung examples are nearly identical under both). The pair that diverges most is weighted_renal vs v2_composite (τ 0.87), and the example table shows what the v2 composite buys: for Curry Fish Head it swaps two fish-dominated (high-phosphorus) matches for lower-mineral zi_char proteins — a clinically better trade for CKD patients that a pure nutrient distance cannot see. **Recommendation: keep the v2 composite** for patient-facing suggestions — 'similar but safer' beats 'most similar', and the category guard is what stops nonsense like a soup replacing a drink. The one worthwhile upgrade is to swap v2's unweighted nutrient term for the renal-weighted distance: with τ 0.92 against v1 it is a low-risk change that makes the underlying distance itself clinically meaningful.

## Adopted changes

Both recommendations above have been adopted in production code (`backend/mining/`); regression tests live in `backend/mining/test_mining.py`:

* **Trend detection — hybrid Theil-Sen flag / EWMA de-escalation** (`trends.py`). Flags are still raised by the Theil-Sen rule, so the flag set (and the parity check above) is unchanged; severity is de-escalated to `review` when the EWMA-weighted slope (half-life 2 months, now `trends._ewma_slope` and exposed per flag as `ewma_slope_per_month`) no longer satisfies the same flag rule — the plateau behaviour behind EWMA's 6% vs 14% plateau FP edge. Test: `TestTrends::test_plateau_downgraded_by_ewma`.
* **Substitutions — renal-weighted nutrient term in the v2 composite** (`substitutions.py`). The v2 composite now ranks by `renal_nutrient_distance` (Na/K/P ×2) instead of the plain euclidean term; category compatibility, cuisine bonus, healthier-choice prior and tiebreaks are unchanged. Test: `TestSubstitutions::test_v2_uses_renal_weighted_distance`.

*Note:* the `v2_composite` column in section 3 reflects the composite as studied (plain euclidean nutrient term), so the comparison stays reproducible as originally run; production v2 differs by the adopted renal weighting.

# Retrieval benchmark

Generated by `python -m backend.app.chat.retrieval_bench`.

## Protocol

The same 275 chunks (61 rules, 214 dishes), synonym expansion and deterministic tie-breaks are used across methods. Experimental refusal gates are selected on 12 disjoint development questions, then frozen before scoring 58 held-out queries. The deployed BM25 baseline retains its existing threshold. Its historical calibration was not independent of this query set.

RRF combines the union of BM25 and character-ngram candidates (top 50 each, k=60). Held-out score overlap is diagnostic only and never used to retune the gate.

## Decision

Ranking winner: **bm25_word_syn**. Keep BM25 with synonyms in production. Fusion loses overall ranking quality and does not improve typo hit@1. The held-out fusion score distributions overlap, so a single threshold does not cleanly separate relevance.

- bm25_word_syn: 11/11 irrelevant probes refused.
- bm25_rrf_char: 11/11 irrelevant probes refused.

## Limitations

Small, internally authored development/test sets and semantic relevance labels do not establish clinical validity. Some end-to-end questions overlap ranking questions; those are complementary metrics, not independent evidence. Q5 separately checks patient applicability, clinician overrides and exact approved values. New runs write full per-query results, thresholds and development examples to `artifacts/retrieval/retrieval_bench_results.json` (or the configured artifact root), not this source directory.

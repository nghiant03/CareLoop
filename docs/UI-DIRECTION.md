# Clinical workspace direction

Mode: Operate. Scope: all frontend screens, with Knowledge Review as the primary
workspace redesign. Confirmed by the user: clinician-first and hospital
wayfinding-inspired, with deep blue navigation and cool neutral surfaces.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Evidence and decisions take precedence over report decoration. Keep
familiar navigation and a list/detail workspace for inspecting knowledge.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy shell, blue selection, white working surfaces and cool slate
ground. Public Sans for reading; tabular numerals for measurements. Sentence-case
labels, modest corners, consistent bordered controls and explicit state text.

STORY: Identify the pinned draft, find a rule, read its source and mapping, inspect
blockers, then record a revision-bound decision.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shared branded header, title and draft status, compact revision
summary, section navigation, then a searchable left rule list and wider right
evidence area. The selected row has a persistent marker and matching detail title.
Decision remains a distinct section; evidence is the first working surface.

FORM: User-confirmed hospital wayfinding direction; seed 154056e8. Bright ambient
clinical use calls for light reading surfaces beneath a dark navigation band.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
